ACCOUNT TYPE
Personal means publishing as an individual, such as a hobbyist or independent
developer. Organization means publishing for a business or organization. Both
can monetize and invite collaborators. Google generally requires a D-U-N-S
number to verify an organization. This is a Play Console ownership choice,
not a free-versus-paid choice and not a Windows-versus-Android build setting.
https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13634885
