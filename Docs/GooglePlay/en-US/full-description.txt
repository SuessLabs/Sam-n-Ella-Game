Sam n' Ella is a single-player pixel-art platformer with strange enemies, unusual weapons, and a very questionable sense of humor.

Choose Sam or Ella, jump across platforms, collect coins, and take on boss fights. Explore outdoor stages and descend through pipes into the Underworld, an underground sewer filled with sludge and trouble.

WHEN YOU HAVE TO GO, YOU HAVE TO GO
Speed Runs stages put your timing to the test. Collect pink bottles to keep the countdown going, use special pipes for extra time, and reach the toilet before time runs out.

RETRO ACTION ON YOUR PHONE
- Play as Sam or Ella.
- Fight with weapons including the shotgun and chain whip.
- Face crawling worms, circling flies, bottle-throwing punks, and bosses.
- Use on-screen directional, jump, attack, and pause controls.
- Chase high scores on a local leaderboard.
- Adjust music and sound-effect volumes.
- Play offline without a game account.

CONTENT NOTICE
Contains cartoon violence, pixel-art blood and gore, crude toilet humor, insulting dialogue, and depictions of alcohol and syringes.
